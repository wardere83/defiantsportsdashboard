Where NIL stands, in one place.

    What is NIL?

    Short for Name, Image, and Likeness, NIL is how college athletes can now receive compensation. A brand "uses" an athlete's NIL when it leverages their name, image, or likeness in marketing and promotion.

    The NCAA made it legal for athletes to profit from their NILs on July 1, 2021. States have since layered on their own rules — and universities their own programs — which is why the map above matters.

    Defiant Sports exists to make that landscape workable. We source, vet, and structure endorsements so athletes keep their equity, brands know exactly what they're getting, and every deal survives an audit.
